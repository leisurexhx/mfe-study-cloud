# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility,
and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, publishing private information, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct
issues privately to the repository owner until a dedicated moderation address is published.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
